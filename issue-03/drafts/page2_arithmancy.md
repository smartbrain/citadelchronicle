### I. The Incubation Decree of St. Mungo’s

When the great Spattergroit Panic of 2024 threatened to overwhelm the apothecaries of Diagon Alley, the Wizengamot acted with characteristic bureaucratic heroism. Bypassing four centuries of herbal safety trials, the Ministry of Health authorised the emergency distribution of the **Mandatory Pepperup-Dragonblood Elixir (Batch 4.2)**, commanding every wizard, witch, and registered squib to present their upper arm at the nearest dispensary.

Yet even the most zealous ministerial alchemists acknowledged a minor physiological technicality: magical humors do not transmute instantaneously. A draught requires time to circulate through the spleen, align with the celestial moon phase, and coat the ethereal organs in protective mucus.

To accommodate this biological lag, St. Mungo’s Hospital promulgated **Decree No. 714 (On the Temporal Classification of Inoculated Persons)**:

> *“Any citizen who imbibes the Draught shall, for a period of exactly fourteen solar days following administration, be legally, metaphysically, and statistically deemed **Wholly Un-Draughted**.”*

On paper, the rule appeared the very model of administrative prudence. If a wizard were struck down by Spattergroit on Day 3, it would be terribly unfair to blame the elixir; the magical shield had simply not had time to solidify.

It was only when the death certificates began rolling into the Ministry’s subterranean statistical chambers that the true genius of the fourteen-day rule revealed itself.

### II. The Miracle of the Shifting Denominator

Human biology is a messy, unpredictable medium. While the majority of recipients merely suffered mild dizziness, purple tongue, and occasional levitation, a non-trivial cohort experienced more pronounced adverse reactions. 

Some patients, within forty-eight hours of receiving the jab, spontaneously sprouted basilisk scales. Others suffered catastrophic kidney petrification, exploded into small clouds of sulfurous brimstone, or collapsed directly onto the apothecary floorboards with their wands still smoking.

Under ordinary arithmetic, such unfortunate outcomes might have raised awkward questions regarding the toxicity of the dragonblood stabiliser. But under the divine protection of Decree No. 714, a miraculous statistical alchemy occurred.

Because the deceased had passed away within the sacred two-week incubation window, the attending Healers were legally forbidden from classifying them as “Draughted.” 

With a swift stroke of a peacock quill, every collapsed patient was dutifully logged into the great parchment ledger under the official heading:  
**“TRAGIC CASUALTY AMONG THE UN-DRAUGHTED POPULATION.”**

The resulting newspaper headlines were breathtaking in their moral clarity:  
*“Spattergroit Slaughter Continues Unabated Among Stubborn Unvaccinated Heretics!”*  
*“St. Mungo’s Mortuary Full of Defiant Fools Who Delayed Their Inoculation!”*

If a wizard perished while walking out of the clinic with the empty vial still clutched in his stiffening fingers, his death was heralded across the *Daily Prophet* as incontrovertible scientific evidence of how lethal it was to remain unprotected.

### III. The Inverse Toxicity Theorem

In the quiet corridors of the Department of Magical Arithmancy, young statisticians quickly realized they had stumbled upon the greatest mathematical perpetual-motion machine in the history of public health.

The theorem was breathtakingly simple: **The worse the medicine, the higher its statistical efficacy.**

Consider the mathematics:
1. Every patient killed by the potion between Day 0 and Day 13 is added to the **Unvaccinated** death toll, artificially inflating the apparent lethality of the disease among the non-compliant.
2. Every patient killed by the potion between Day 0 and Day 13 is censored from the **Vaccinated** cohort, leaving the survivors of Day 14 as a pristine, pre-filtered sample of exceptionally hardy constitutions.
3. Therefore, as the immediate lethality of the Draught increases, the calculated *Relative Risk Reduction* soars towards perfection!

In an extraordinary confidential memo recently leaked from the Wizengamot, a senior Arithmancer laid out the mathematical proof:

> *“Noble Lords, our calculations indicate that if we intentionally double the concentration of powdered boomslang skin so that 98% of recipients perish within seventy-two hours, the mortality rate among the Un-Draughted will approach mathematical infinity. Simultaneously, the mortality rate among the post-Day-14 cohort will be zero, because only immortal demigods will survive to cross the threshold. We shall achieve 100.00% certified clinical protection, earning St. Mungo’s the international gold standard in epidemiological excellence.”*

The Chief Healer was so moved by the logic that he awarded the author an Order of Merlin, Second Class, and ordered another four million doses from the conglomerate’s cauldron facilities.

### IV. The Citadel Commentary

Reviewing the St. Mungo’s epidemiological scrolls over his morning flat white at the Citadel Tavern, Lung Chaloke shook his head with weary amusement.

“When a fiat central bank enters terminal insolvency, it alters the basket of the Consumer Price Index to prove that beef has never been cheaper,” he remarked, brushing aside a crumb of sourdough. “And when fiat medicine poisons the populace, the state alters the denominator to prove the poison is life itself.

“In both cases, the grift relies entirely on the settlement delay. The central bankers hide the inflation behind lagged statistics; the apothecaries hide the casualties behind a fourteen-day grace period. By the time the ledger reconciles, the architects have collected their bonuses and retired to their country manors in Surrey.

“Do not argue with their arithmancers. Do not submit your spleen to their administrative decrees. Stack physical satoshis, cultivate your own biological immunity, and remember: when the state tells you that dying with an empty vial in your hand makes you an unvaccinated casualty, it is time to build your own hospital outside the city walls.”
