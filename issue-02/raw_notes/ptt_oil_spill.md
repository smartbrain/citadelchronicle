# PRIVATISED GAINS, SOCIALISED LOSSES
**Author**: Don Sambandaraksa  
**Topic**: PTT Global Chemical 2013 Rayong Oil Spill Supreme Court Verdict  
**Date Logged**: October 2026  

Right on cue for the column, the fat cats at PTT Global Chemical have officially washed their hands of the ghastly 2013 Rayong oil spill. Thirteen years after they completely mucked up the coastline, the Supreme Court has let the petrochemical giant off the hook for banking a dedicated environmental clean-up fund. The beak’s verdict? Let the state ministries—and by extension, the public purse—mop up the mess.

Meanwhile, the 400-odd local fishermen and traders whose livelihoods were blackened by the slick have been fobbed off with the exact same paltry payouts decided years ago. They’ll be making do with a measly 90,000 to 150,000 Baht a head, minus the 30-grand sop they were already tossed as an advance. Once again, the big boys pocket the profits and skip the bill, while the working bloke is left entirely high and dry.
